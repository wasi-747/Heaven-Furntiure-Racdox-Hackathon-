PROVENANCE & TIMBER SCIENCE

Built Upon Generational Woodcraft & Integrity

Founded in 2020 by Abul Kalam Bhuiyan, Heaven Furniture Mart emerged from a single uncompromising belief: that discerning Bengali homeowners deserve furniture custom-measured to their floorplans, handcrafted from genuinely seasoned solid hardwood, and built to outlast generations.

THE FOUNDER'S VISION

A Message From Abul Kalam Bhuiyan

"When I established Heaven Furniture Mart on Agrabad Access Road in 2020, I noticed that homeowners across Chattogram were trapped between two extremes: cheap, disposable particle-board furniture that buckles within two rainy seasons, or rigid corporate catalogs that refuse to adapt to the unique architectural dimensions of high-end duplexes.

We chose a third, uncompromising path: The Architectural Atelier. Here, no wood is cut until we understand your ceiling height, your natural daylight, and your family's daily rituals. We personally hand-select every log of Burmese teak and Gamari, season it scientifically, and stand behind it with an ironclad 10-year guarantee."

SCIENTIFIC RIGOR

The Timber Science Lab

Why do Heaven pieces never warp, crack, or squeak in Chattogram's coastal humidity? Because we treat wood seasoning as an exact engineering science.

GRADE-A HARDWOOD

Burmese Teak

Naturally rich in protective natural tectoquinones and organic oils. Repels water droplets and creates an impenetrable defense against termites without artificial lacquers.

  • ✓ Density: 650–750 kg/m³
  • ✓ High natural oil content
  • ✓ Golden honey patina that deepens with age
STABLE INDIGENOUS TIMBER

Chittagong Gamari

Sourced ethically from the Chittagong Hill Tracts. Celebrated for its fine, uniform interlocking grain structure that exhibits virtually zero dimensional shrinkage or twisting.

  • ✓ Density: 480–560 kg/m³
  • ✓ Exceptional resistance to warp
  • ✓ Ideal for sleek modern vanities & doors
ARCHITECTURAL TIMBER

Smoked Hardwood

Undergoes deep thermal fuming to achieve a rich, chocolate-espresso tone throughout the timber core. Delivers an understated European architectural aesthetic.

  • ✓ Density: 610–680 kg/m³
  • ✓ Deep thermal coloring
  • ✓ Preferred for C-Suite director suites
