Transparent Timber Science & Customization

Client Query Clarifier

Direct, honest answers to the questions discerning patrons ask before commissioning bespoke architectural furniture in Chattogram.

We strictly uphold a 100% solid hardwood guarantee. Heaven Furniture Mart never uses MDF, processed particle board, or thin commercial veneers in any load-bearing or visible frame. Every structural component is carved from mature Grade-A Burmese Teak or Chittagong Gamari hardwood logs.

Before polishing coats are applied, clients are warmly invited to inspect raw unpolished timber cross-sections at our Agrabad workshop to verify the natural ring density and genuine grain with their own eyes.

  • ✓ Zero Processed MDF or Particle Board
  • ✓ 100% Seasoned Solid Hardwood Logs
  • ✓ Raw Workshop Inspection Welcome

Guaranteed zero seasonal warping. Chattogram's coastal climate often reaches 80% to 90% atmospheric humidity during monsoon season. Standard furniture swells, twists, and cracks because raw timber holds uneven internal moisture.

At Heaven, every single timber plank undergoes thermal kiln-seasoning and dehumidification chambers, reducing core moisture below 10–12%. This locks cellular walls permanently and eliminates seasonal movement, ensuring drawers slide effortlessly and joint lines remain flush for decades.

  • ✓ Sub-10% Kiln-Stabilized Moisture
  • ✓ Immune to Coastal Air Expansion
  • ✓ Lifetime Interlocking Mortise Joinery

Yes, full dimensional customization is the core of our atelier. Unlike standard retail showrooms that sell fixed sizes, every Heaven commission is custom-dimensioned to your ceiling height, door clearance, and floor geometry.

Bring your architect's blueprint or request our complimentary on-site measurement service anywhere in Chattogram. We generate photorealistic 3D CAD room layouts so you can approve proportions and circulation before a single chisel touches timber.

  • ✓ Centimeter-Exact Room Sizing
  • ✓ Complimentary 3D CAD Blueprints
  • ✓ Free In-Home Measurement in CTG

Every delivered commission arrives with an official, signed 10-Year Guarantee Certificate issued directly by Managing Director Abul Kalam Bhuiyan. This covers frame structural integrity, mortise joint stability, and zero termite infestation.

Our timber is pressure-infused with anti-borer and anti-fungal treatments. Furthermore, as official registered members of the Chittagong Chamber of Commerce & Industry (CCCI) and BFIOA, our warranty is an enforceable corporate bond.

  • ✓ Signed 10-Year Written Guarantee
  • ✓ Anti-Termite Replacement Bond
  • ✓ Member, Chittagong Chamber (CCCI)

We follow a transparent 4-stage architectural commissioning workflow:

1. Blueprint Curation: Select pieces via our Room Blueprint drawer or send your interior designer's CAD plan.
2. Consultation & Sampling: Confirm wood species (Teak / Gamari), stain tones, and fabrics at our Agrabad Studio or via WhatsApp VIP Concierge.
3. 50% Production Advance: Hand-carving and joinery begin in our workshop. Typical lead time is 4 to 8 weeks.
4. White-Glove Installation: Our senior craftsmen deliver, level, and assemble every piece in your residence. Final 50% payment is cleared only upon your full inspection and satisfaction.

  • ✓ 50% Milestone Advance
  • ✓ 50% Post-Assembly Inspection
  • ✓ 4–8 Weeks Precision Handcraft
